Questions

Membership FAQ

Do I have to be a member to see Dr. Khan?

No. Membership is optional. Dr. Khan sees patients through most PPO plans and Medicare. Membership adds direct access, faster appointments, an annual physical with labs and a yearly house call.

Does membership replace my insurance?

No. Keep your insurance for hospital care, specialists and anything outside the office. Labs and imaging can be billed through your plan or offered at discounted rates.

How do I request a house call?

Call 657.218.9859 or email info@occonciergedoctor.com with the reason for the visit and a phone number where we can reach you.

Are families eligible?

Yes. Membership is available to individuals and families, with family enrollment discounts.

How do I join?

Call the office at 657.218.9859 to book your first appointment and enroll.
