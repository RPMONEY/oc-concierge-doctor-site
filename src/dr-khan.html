His approach

Your needs, first.

Dr. Khan practices as a primary care physician dedicated to putting his patients' medical needs first. His focus is preventive care that keeps you well, backed by the training to evaluate serious illness critically and build a treatment plan around the individual.

People across Tustin and Orange County count on him for everything from immediate help to the long-term management of chronic conditions. Alongside primary and urgent care, he offers weight loss and anti-aging services and runs a full-service aesthetic clinic.

His concierge model brings back something medicine lost: a doctor you can actually reach, appointments without the wait, and house calls when you need them.
